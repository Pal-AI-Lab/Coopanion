## Unreleased

- The right-click menu shows only the actions picked as hover buttons (`hoverButtons`), beside the run controls the app lends; the actions not picked are no longer listed there. `hoverButtons` may be empty (0–6, it was 1–6), from the settings window and from `pet_set`: no buttons on hover, and a menu of the run controls alone, or none without them.
- A pet dialog takes `{ kind: 'pick', options, value?, min?, max, confirm }`: cards of which `min` (1 when absent) to `max` are picked, answered as `{ picked: [index, …] }` in the cards' order, holding only indices of its own cards.
- `PET_ACTIONS`, `MAX_HOVER_BUTTONS`, `hoverButtonList` and the type `PetAction` are exported.
- Tool `pet_see_self` (no arguments): a PNG of the body as the pet window draws it at the moment of the call, over a grey backdrop, without the page's bubbles and buttons, with a line naming the figure and its dress. The World sends the page a `portrait` order; the page answers `portrait` with `{ png, width, height }` (from `petHost.portrait(rect)`, `host/portrait.cjs`) or `{ failed }` (`tab`, `hidden`, `no-body`, `capture`). Figure packs take no part in it.
- Each figure keeps its own size: the config's `figureScales` holds, per pack id, the `window.scale` a figure had when another replaced it, and a figure put on takes its own back unless the size changed with it. `POST /api/prefs` takes `scale`, and `POST /api/skin` an optional `scale` to put the look on at. Saved looks keep an optional `scale` (`SavedLook.scale`); one without it goes on at its figure's own size, or at the size on screen when the figure has none.
- `size` is a pet action (`PET_ACTIONS`, `hoverButtons`): hovering its button shows a size slider over the body. The dressing page has a size row under the figures. Both draw the slider of `web/scale-slider.js`.
- A describer's `PetStatus` may carry `countdown`, the seconds a call lasts. When that call starts running (RunPhase `running`), `StatusTracker` sends `endsAt` in its place, the Date.now() milliseconds the countdown ends, and only while the call runs; the page shows the whole seconds left after `text`, out of what screen readers read. A describer that sets no `countdown` behaves as before. `waitSeconds(seconds)` is exported: how long `pet_cua_wait` waits for an argument, 0–30, 1 when absent.
- Sound kind `alert` (`worlds.desktop-pet.sounds.alert`, on by default) plays once as a confirmation bubble shows. Packs cannot file sounds under it.
- A click on a line's bubble, away from its links, ends the whole line and the page reports `skipped` with the line's id; questions and confirmations still close only by their × or an answer.
- While the pet window is hidden the page ticks the body with `draw` false (`tick { dt, draw }`, `step(dt, draw)`): the body moves on without drawing, so walks still arrive. `kit.createBody` honours it; a pack that ignores it draws as before.
- `kit.createBody(host, { ground: 'free' })` builds a body that stands anywhere on the stage above the floor, seen from above: it walks to any point, is set down where it is let go of, and a throw tosses it across the stage. Such a body reports `y` (the stage y its feet stand on) in `layout()`, in `arrived` and `interrupted`; the World keeps it with the remembered position (`petY`, hands it back as `start.y` and `init.startY`) and names it in `pet_move_to`'s receipt. `place(x, facing, y)` takes an optional `y`. Bodies without `y` behave as before.
- `kit.createBody` answers the pack's own targets through `opts.moves`: `{ id: (params, bounds) => ({ x, y? }) | null }`.
- Every body hears `set({ status })`, the kind of work the status bubble shows (or null); the kit hands it to the figure as `frame.status`.
- figure.json may add `voice` (`{ pitch }`, 0.5–2), sounds of kind `talk` (played letter by letter in place of the synthesized babble), `effects` (`{ reach }`, how far past its box a pack draws, in 256-unit squares, 0–2: the halo's window and the OBS overlay's capture grow by it) and `bubble` (`{ light, dark }` of `{ fill, text, line }`, on the pack or a preset) for the bot's bubbles. Older apps skip all of them; `/api/figures` lists `voice`, `effects` and `bubble`.
- A sound in figure.json may set `repeat`, what happens when it is asked for while its last play has not ended: `restart` fades that play out over 30 ms and plays from the start, `finish` leaves this one out, `overlap` (the default) plays over it. `talk` sounds ignore it; a value this version does not know is named in the scan's problems and plays as `overlap`. Older apps play every sound as `overlap`.

- `HOST_ENV` and `HOST_MAIN` are internal to the window host and no longer exported. Embedding apps configure the command with `CORTICO_DESKTOP_PET_HOST`.
- An app can lend the bot's name as `botName()` in `desktopPetDefinition`'s assembly; it is read at each use, so the menu header, the chat page (a `bot` frame) and computer use's question follow a rename without a World restart. Without it the World keeps the name it was mounted with. `DesktopPetWorldOptions.botName` and `CuaOptions.botName` are functions now.
- `pet_walk_to` is now `pet_move_to`, with the same `to` and `run` and an optional `params`. A bot that names the old tool has to use the new name.
- Figure packs may declare targets of their own for it (`moves` in figure.json): the World checks the call's params against the pack and the body does the move (`move(args, id)`), reporting `arrived` or `interrupted` as walks do. The bot reads the targets in `{{pet.moves}}` and hears of added and removed ones on a figure switch. Packs without `moves`, Coo and the whale among them, behave as before.
- A pack that `needs` `windows` gets the visible top edges of the other windows on the pet's display in `host.bounds().ledges`, and those windows whole in `host.bounds().windows` (front first, each with an opaque id and, except on macOS, whether it is the foreground window), on Windows, macOS and Linux under X11 (the last two not yet tried on a machine); the window process reads none for other packs. `petHost.onWindows` hands the page `{ ledges, windows }`.
- The window host's `--pet-url` is `http://127.0.0.1:<port>/pet#key=<credential>`, the credential random. A host set by `CORTICO_DESKTOP_PET_HOST` passes the URL on and loads it unchanged, the part after `#` included. `petUrl`, `petState().url` and the panel's links carry no credential.
- The pet window's socket (`host=window`) and the OBS overlay source (`role=overlay-source`) put that credential in the `/socket` query as `key`; a socket without it or with another one is answered 403.
- Browser writes (every method but GET and HEAD) and the WebSocket are accepted only with the service's own HTTP origin, port included (`http://127.0.0.1:<port>` or `http://localhost:<port>`); any other Origin is answered 421. A client that sends no Origin is accepted.
- `POST /api/figures/import/install` answers 409 with `{ conflicts: [id, …] }` when a chosen pack's id is installed or `figures/<id>/` exists, and then changes no file and keeps the staged import. The same request with the same `token` and `replace: [id, …]` naming every conflicting id replaces them. An install without conflicts takes no new field. Once an install passes these checks its token is spent.
