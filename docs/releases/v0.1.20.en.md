# Coopanion v0.1.20

**11 interface languages, OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini and xAI added, voice input in French, German, Spanish, Portuguese, Italian and Russian, and figure pack import on the Dress up page**

## New

- **11 languages**: 简体中文, 繁體中文, English, 日本語, 한국어, Français, Deutsch, Español (Latinoamérica), Português (Brasil), Italiano and Русский. A new install uses the system language (English when it is none of these); an upgraded install keeps its language. Switch it in the first row of the Habits page; the tray, the pet and the settings window change at once, without a restart.
- **Coo speaks your language**: Coo reads its persona and prompts in Chinese when the app language is Simplified or Traditional Chinese and in English otherwise; for languages other than Simplified Chinese and English, Coo is also told to talk to you in the app language. An unedited starting persona switches to the other version from the next session; an edited one stays as it is.
- **More model services**: OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini and xAI. Kimi, Qwen, MiniMax and Zhipu GLM gain their international platforms; a new connection uses the mainland China platform when the app language is Simplified Chinese and the international one otherwise, and the Start page switches between them. Services are ordered by language; outside Simplified Chinese, Doubao, Baidu Qianfan and StepFun are under **More**.
- **Voice input in French, German, Spanish, Portuguese, Italian and Russian**: these languages are recognized on your machine by Whisper (small model, about 360 MB), with the text appearing once a sentence ends. Chinese, English, Japanese and Korean keep FunASR.
- **Figure pack import**: **Import** at the end of the figure row on the Dress up page installs a pack from a zip or a folder; dropping one on the page works too. Packs written for figure API 2 keep loading in later versions.
- **Guide**: a new "Where did you hear about me?" question; talking to Coo before a model is connected offers to connect one.
- **Computer use asks once by default**: new installs ask before the first mouse or keyboard action and not again for 30 minutes after a yes; existing installs keep asking every turn.

## Fixes

- **StepFun and Qwen failed to parse with thinking on**: their streamed reasoning events are converted to standard events before validation.
- **Qwen, StepFun, Baidu Qianfan and Zhipu's mainland platform could return 400 during computer use**: their tool results take text only, so screenshots go in the message right after.
- **Baidu Qianfan could not read images**: it now goes through Chat Completions, and the default model is the vision-capable `glm-5.3-flash`.
- **"No thinking" failed on the Zhipu GLM-5.3 series**: the series cannot turn thinking off, so the lowest level is sent.
- **Coo did not learn that the vocabulary changed after a figure switch**: it is now told which expressions and motions are gone and which are new.

## Download

- Windows x64: `Coopanion-Setup-0.1.20.exe`
- Apple silicon Mac: `Coopanion-0.1.20-mac-arm64.dmg` / `.zip`
- Intel Mac: `Coopanion-0.1.20-mac-x64.dmg` / `.zip`
- Linux x64: `Coopanion-0.1.20-linux-x64.deb` / `.AppImage`
- `SHA256SUMS.txt`: SHA-256 checksums of the seven installers.
- `latest.yml`, `latest-linux.yml`: version information read by auto-update; no need to download.

The Windows build and the AppImage update to this version automatically. The macOS build is still ad-hoc signed and not notarized by Apple; see the README for opening it the first time. Translations other than Simplified Chinese and English are machine-translated; corrections are welcome as pull requests.
